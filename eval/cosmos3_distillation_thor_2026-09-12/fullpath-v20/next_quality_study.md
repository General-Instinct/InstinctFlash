# Proposed matched gradient-quality study (not scheduled)

V20 established exact native derivative/optimizer/export feasibility. Nano final64 did not recover UniPC4 quality, and Edge V17 last-callback final64 also failed its short-action comparison. The next unresolved question is whether changing only the Edge SDE2 gradient estimator improves final64 action quality at the same update budget. This document is a prospective design for review, not an execution protocol, new training authorization, RNG reservation, or automatic follow-on to V20.

## Four fresh arms

Use two new paired training seeds, each with a last-callback control and complete-path candidate. Compress must choose actual seeds and freeze expanded reservations against all previous studies before any execution. Use explicit pair IDs distinct from arm IDs; maintain separate initialization/training/probe/export reservations per pair, including disjointness from V20. Evaluation uses intentionally inherited paired-policy requests, with separate historical teacher32 draws, rather than newly reserved evaluation seeds. Within each pair verify initial master tensors and pre-student warmup states are identical. After student1, fake states and scores may diverge because each fake tracks its own student; do not force post-treatment equality.

Each arm independently initializes student, fake, teacher and optimizers from the same released original Edge checkpoint; pair initial LoRA draws and data/noise schedules within seed. No pilot, V17, V16 or Nano weights, warmed fake states, optimizer states or EMA snapshots are reused.

The only treatment is V20's tested derivative rule. Hold student SDE2 [1,.5,0], CFG1, prefix1/action2, native precision, trainable last4/rank16 scope/action heads, teacher CFG4 (one score query with two branches), fake CFG1 (one conditional branch per score query), padding, prompt/metadata, history1/future32, loss normalization/clipping and optimizer settings fixed to the frozen V20/V17 recipe. Both rules must still reproduce their own independent native VJP oracle. Apply the derivative treatment only during student updates. Fake updates use detached student endpoints and generation gradient scope none; DMD teacher/fake targets remain no-grad. EMA may be retained diagnostically but is not evaluated or exported. More or different teacher queries, LoRA scope, horizon or distillation loss would confound the comparison and belong in another study.

The frozen V20 executable is deliberately restricted to two arms, one training seed and two training contexts; changing update counts alone is insufficient. A future separately reviewed formal implementation must support four arms and the original V17 training panel (32 training contexts), preserve its32-context membership/order/dataset identities and the separate16 development contexts, with fresh paired preparation draws, and generalize its admission, audit, export and source-binding checks without editing V20. The V19 evaluator also assumes old seed/arm names and an11-bank catalog; create separate seven-bank capture/analysis/audit adapters instead of weakening those guards. Do not train64 updates by repeatedly using only the two pilot contexts. This design does not supply or authorize that executable implementation.

Per arm use the V17 formal budget:128 fake warmup updates,64 student updates,5 fake updates between consecutive student updates (315 continuing;443 fake total). Use terminal online64 only. Preserve diagnostic snapshots0/1/4/16/64 and V17's all-parameter audit sentinels, but do not select a checkpoint from quality results. All four arms run the same number of updates; measure wall time/memory separately. This is an update-budget comparison, not an equal-compute comparison. No automatic extension beyond64 if quality is poor.

## Compute accounting to freeze before launch

Under the unchanged native callback recipe, each arm generates507 complete two-step endpoints (1014 conditional generation branches),128 teacher CFG branches (64 queries ×2), and507 conditional fake-score branches:1649 training branches per arm,6596 across four arms. Repeating the two V20 probes per paired seed costs80 branches total; four export/cold gates cost24. This predicts6700 training/probe/export branches, conditional on source reconciliation. With1024 additional student quality branches, the full proposed new-compute total is7724. Preserve2028 raw RF/DMD field sets and48 complete Adam sentinels (student1/4/16/64, warmup1/4/16/128, continuing fake1/4/16/315 per arm). Count any failed or partial calls separately and stop rather than exceeding the frozen budget. The executable protocol must derive and check these counts; they are not measured usage or permission to exceed them.

The single-H100 fit is established by the pilot, but formal throughput is not. Do not use instrumented update wall time with audit I/O to promise runtime. Only Compress schedules H1004–7 after an actual idle/ownership check, with one physical GPU visible per process; services0–3 and Thor remain untouched. No new worker is scheduled by this document.

## Quality panel and pairing

The three inherited reports are `twostep_v17/evaluation_capture_v19/original_same_grid/report.json`, `paired_training_v13/evaluation_capture/original_unipc4/report.json`, and `paired_training_v13/evaluation_capture/teacher_unipc32/report.json` under the Compress results/cosmos3_droid root. The CPU review verified384 request JSONs and384 referenced NPZ hashes; this is compatibility evidence, not future new-student reuse admission.

Primary causal comparison: complete-path online64 versus its paired last-callback online64, averaged over the two training seeds within each episode. Report each seed separately as well. Retain original same-grid SDE2 and original UniPC4 baselines, plus a separate original teacher32 reference. Teacher32 is a distributional reference, not ground truth.

Use the existing frozen16-episode ×K8 historical development panel to make results comparable. Reuse the exact original Edge control banks only after their full pre-projection inputs, request seeds, targets, checkpoint identities, declared clocks/padding, prompt/metadata, action normalization and external Wan dependency are verified against the new study. The released UniPC controls preserve their original native padding; the SDE2 student/control use the frozen zero-padding contract. Do not rewrite or homogenize these captured inputs. Last-versus-full SDE2 is the estimator-isolation comparison because both arms share schedule and padding. UniPC4 is a whole-operating-point reference, with both sampler and padding differences, not an estimator-only causal control. Do not substitute Nano controls. Four new student banks require512 fresh requests/1024 generation branches. With all three original control banks retained, analysis covers896 requests. If reuse gates fail, stop and document the incompatibility before prescribing a separately bounded capture; do not silently regenerate or change the panel. All-seven-bank recapture would cost10496 branches and is not part of this design.

Freeze15 contrasts: three estimator contrasts (two seed-specific and one seed mean) plus12 treatment/reference contrasts (two treatments ×two references ×three seed summaries). Each contrast reports all four primary metrics. Predeclare raw action energy score for jointH1, gripperH1, jointH32 and gripperH32; lower is better. These primary energy scores are against recorded commands; teacher32 comparisons are separate diagnostics. Preserve full per-request32×8 actions and all96 existing secondary metrics. Use paired episode bootstrap (5000 replicates), computing each seed's energy score separately from its own K8 bank and then averaging those scores within episode; never concatenate both seeds into a K16 distribution; report both absolute differences and percentages against a fixed baseline mean. Intervals remain exploratory and unadjusted across metrics; two training seeds are not a population estimate of training variability. This reused development panel cannot establish generalization, noninferiority or closed-loop task success.

Do not select a winner from one favorable metric. Report all four gradient-control comparisons alongside all four UniPC4 comparisons and their intervals. A result that improves over last-callback but still worsens UniPC4 is a useful estimator finding, not quality recovery. A mixed or null result remains recorded without automatic retries, budget extension, checkpoint selection or promotion. Any later equivalence claim needs a predeclared practical margin and independent held-out evaluation, neither supplied here.

## Admission and deployment boundaries

Bind the exact Wan blob and resolver source explicitly before execution, along with the inherited protocol identity. Before training: validate fresh role/RNG pairing, native complete-grid endpoints,52-parameter matching-rule VJPs, nonzero intermediate-state sensitivity and cut-path control on the two fixed probes per seed. After training: unchanged independent RF/DMD and all-parameter Adam audit, final64 native merge/export, full-field source/native parity, separate cold exactness and external Wan binding. Retain failure evidence; no automatic retry. Preserve V20 and all earlier artifacts.

Serving must use the existing additive realtime adapter capability, not change the public four-step/CFG4 plugin contract. Current V17 package admission is schema/seed/arm-specific; any future final64 copy needs separately versioned provenance guards, sidecar identity and its own source/cold/Flash checks. Keep it servable=false until qualified.

After native quality analysis, assess whether a separate paired Thor numerical screen is worthwhile. Current Edge final64 SDE2 cuDNN latency is about442–444ms on the earlier V17 weights; that does not predict improved quality for new weights. A complete-path estimator changes training, not inference NFE. Nano715–717ms is a different model and cannot serve as its timing baseline.

Actual realtime admission also needs the controller's deadline, action execution chunk, sensor/transport/preprocessing budget and latency-tail acceptance criteria, followed by the appropriate closed-loop evaluation. They are currently unspecified. A model's offline cycle p50 alone does not establish realtime. Keep all prospective students unqualified until their own evidence supports a specific deployment contract.
